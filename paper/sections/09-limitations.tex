\section{Limitations and future work}
\label{sec:limitations}

The fully connected continuous-time result has a deliberately narrow scope.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item The state space is finite. Countably infinite and general measurable state spaces are not covered by the continuous-time generator-to-path-law bridge.
  \item The horizon is finite, and non-explosion is proved as disappearance of the finite-horizon jump-count tail. The project does not construct a single infinite-time path law for the general generator.
  \item Driving consists of finitely many piecewise-constant windows. General calendar-time-dependent rates with integrated hazards are not formalized.
  \item Diffusions, Langevin dynamics, stochastic differential equations, and their path measures are outside the present development.
  \item The reverse dynamics and windowwise detailed-balance hypotheses are supplied in the modeled protocol; reverse-kernel existence by disintegration is not a theorem of the continuous-time layer.
\end{itemize}

The most immediate next step is publication rather than further generalization: fix a versioned repository snapshot, archive it with a persistent identifier, and submit the current end-to-end result. Subsequent work can study countable state spaces, genuinely time-inhomogeneous hazards, and diffusion limits without delaying the scoped finite-state artifact.
