\section{Conclusion}

We have presented a Lean~4 construction that closes the chain from finite-state jump generators to global stochastic-thermodynamic fluctuation relations. Explicit fixed-horizon sectors are normalized into a probability measure; the actual terminal and finite-dimensional pushforwards of that measure are identified with the matrix-exponential semigroup; complete window paths are concatenated into global real-time trajectories; and windowwise Gibbs detailed balance yields Crooks, Jarzynski, work-distribution, and average-work second-law theorems on those global laws.

The formalization does not make classical physics new. Its contribution is to make the interfaces between standard pieces explicit, typed, measurable, normalized, and machine checked. Those interfaces---between sector arithmetic and path measures, path measures and semigroups, marked windows and global trajectories, and dynamics and fluctuation identities---are the central result.
