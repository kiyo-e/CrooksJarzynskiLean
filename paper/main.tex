\begin{abstract}
Fluctuation relations for Markov jump processes are commonly written as ratios of trajectory weights, while the normalized trajectory law to which those weights belong, and its identification with the generator semigroup, are left implicit. We give an end-to-end, machine-checked Lean~4 construction for finite-state processes on a finite time horizon. Starting from a conservative generator, we build a normalized measure on finite jump records, prove finite-horizon non-explosion, and identify its terminal and finite-dimensional marginals with the semigroup $\exp(tQ)$. We then concatenate finitely many constant-generator windows separated by instantaneous energy switches. If each window generator satisfies detailed balance with respect to the Gibbs distribution of its current energy landscape, the forward path law and the time-reversed image of the reverse-protocol law obey the path-measure Crooks identity $e^{-\beta W}\,\Pp_F = e^{-\beta\Delta F}\,\Pp_R^{\Theta}$, where $W$ is the inclusive work done on the system. Jarzynski's equality and, for $\beta>0$, the average-work inequality $\langle W\rangle_F \ge \Delta F$ follow on the same constructed path space. The result covers finite state spaces, finite time, and finite stepwise protocols; no new fluctuation identity is claimed.
\end{abstract}
