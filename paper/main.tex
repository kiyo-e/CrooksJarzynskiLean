\begin{abstract}
We formalize finite-state continuous-time jump processes and their nonequilibrium work relations in Lean~4. Starting from a conservative finite-state generator, we construct normalized fixed-horizon path measures by summing explicit jump-count sectors and prove that no probability mass escapes to infinitely many jumps within the horizon. We identify the terminal marginal of the constructed law with the corresponding row of $\exp(TQ)$, derive its finite-dimensional distributions as chronological transition-kernel chains, and measurably concatenate finitely many piecewise-constant protocol windows into a global real-time path law. Under local Gibbs detailed balance, the same global law carries Crooks, Jarzynski, average-work second-law, and work-distribution relations. The contribution is the machine-checked connection from generators to physical fluctuation relations, rather than new fluctuation identities.
\end{abstract}
